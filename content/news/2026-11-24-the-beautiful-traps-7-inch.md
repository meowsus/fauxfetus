Oh boy, you would not _believe_ how excited I am to announce that the original audio from The Beautiful Traps' _Wooden Skull_ has been recovered. 

For probably close to a decade I've been asked more times than I can count by a number of people that I can count on one hand: "why isn't The Beautiful Traps 7-inch on Faux Fetus?"

I'll tell you why, you 1-to-5 people: because no one had the original audio masters. Well, one person did and another person probably did, but it's really hard to get anyone to care enough to spin up some old hard drives just to appease some obsessive dork.

I've been a really squeaky wheel about this over the last few months. Just today I was soliciting anyone with a good turntable to just copy the thing for me so that I could pay a friend to de-ass the transfer. Luckily, none of that was necessary because my favorite, most-unemployed member of that band (inclusive) had seemingly nothing better to do today than to start plugging in old hard drives.

Y'all interested parties should buy him a beer, next time you see him. Buy them all a fucking beer, already. They're just as thirsty as we've been.

What a day!
