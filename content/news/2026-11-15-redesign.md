To welcome our newest and most centennial artist, I decided to redesign the website. This act was fairly trivial because, though I hand-wrote the generative foundation upon which this archive sits, I used AI to handle the redesign for me. It still took about two days of babysitting, but I'm pretty happy with it overall. Don't applaud me, I just yelled at a robot.

So what all went into this redesign, you ask? Well, I'm sure you don't really care, but allow me to use a few of my organs to record the changes.

First, obviously, the site looks different. Some might say better. Some might say "vibe-coded". Some, myself included, don't care and feel like anything was better than that gross, off-the-shelf UI library I unfortunately chose for us all to endure.

Second, this design sits atop a (probably still) completely semantic and accessible set of documents. That should help level out the auditorial impairment of those of us who may be visually impaired.

Third, we have a sexy new player that can be invoked by the header's "radio" link or through an album's track list, as before. This floats at the bottom of the screen and comes with an expandable track-list drawer that appears above the main player.

Fourth, we have a new global search that will fuzzy-match on pretty much anything you throw at it. Have fun with that.

Fifthly, and lastly and just for me, I updated the way that these updates are written. Obviously you're not getting inundated with my banal blather as soon as you land on the site, but are instead displayed a list of artists that have been added since this site's reconception, again, and for the last time, hopefully... but unlikely...

But, if you're ever interested, you can read the news and keep me company.

Hope you like.
